Common questions

I don't hear anything.

Check that your ringer isn't muted all the way down — offthetop plays through the media volume, and it is designed to keep playing even with the silent switch on. Make sure a track actually has steps turned on, and that the track's volume isn't at zero in its instrument panel.

Where do my beats go?

Everything is saved on your phone. Beats you save appear under SAVED in the sequencer toolbar, and your settings, bars notebook, and rhyme-group edits persist automatically between sessions. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.

How do I use my own sounds?

In any track's instrument panel, tap + IMPORT to pick an audio file, or record one directly. You can also drop audio files into the offthetop folder in the iOS Files app and they'll be picked up the next time the app opens. Imported sounds can be trimmed, retuned, and used on any track.

Can I record myself rapping?

Yes — tap the microphone on the Spit screen. You can capture vocals on their own or mixed with the beat, and recordings can be exported out of the app.

Does it need an internet connection?

No. offthetop works entirely offline.

Is offthetop free?

Yes. The whole app is free — every instrument, tool, and feature. There are no in-app purchases, no subscription, and no ads.

What data does it collect?

None. offthetop does not collect any data from this app. See the privacy policy for details.
